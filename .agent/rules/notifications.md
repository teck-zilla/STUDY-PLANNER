# rules/notifications.md
Job: the timing, limits and channels of email and in-app messages. Reminder time is in rules/plan-rules.md, rule 14. Quiet hours, unsubscribe links and the open signal are held in AGENTS.md.

## Rules
1. Use no guilt wording in any message. Why (PRD): guilt wording makes students leave (section 4, a hypothesis).
2. Numbers in a message must be true at send time, for example "5 minutes, 6 cards". Why (reading): a wrong number destroys trust. The hourly job computes the plan first (rules/plan-rules.md, rule 15).
3. Ladder after missed days (FR-13). Why (PRD): reminders must never nag.
   - Day 1 missed: send nothing.
   - Day 2: one email and one in-app message.
   - Day 4: the plan shrinks and a 10 minute catch-up is offered.
   - Day 7: a message says the plan is paused. One tap restarts it.
   - After that: exam countdown emails only, at 14, 7 and 2 days before each exam.
4. Limits: 3 emails in any rolling 7 days. 1 in-app message per day. Why (PRD): reminders must never nag (FR-13).
5. Priority when a limit blocks a message: exam countdown, pause notice, catch-up, reminder. No PRD reason.
6. Two ignored messages in a row stop all emails except exam countdowns. Countdown emails are at most 3 per exam and can be turned off in one tap. Why (reading): it stops nagging a student who is not responding.
7. A response is the student opening the app within 48 hours after a message (PRD 6.0). Ignored means no response. No PRD reason for 48 hours.
8. One tap pauses everything. On resume the plan restarts at the daily target. No PRD reason.
9. If an email bounces, stop emailing and use in-app messages only. A student who returns resets the ladder. No PRD reason.
10. The weekly note is in-app only and never uses email (FR-10). No PRD reason.
11. Exam result prompt (FR-16): ask once in the app 2 days after the exam date. If there is no reply after 7 days, send one email, which counts toward the 3 email limit. Then stop. Why (reading): the result is the record version two needs most (PRD 5.3).
